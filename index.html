Optimal Route Calculator

This app uses OpenStreetMap, Leaflet, and OpenRouteService (ORS) to calculate driving routes. It computes fuel cost based on 2.7 AED per litre and 6.2 km/l fuel consumption. ORS does not provide exact toll prices, so tolls are handled by requesting routes that avoid tolls (if possible) and routes that do not. Fuel cost is calculated for each. The "optimal" route is selected as the one with the lowest fuel cost. You can manually input toll costs if known.
